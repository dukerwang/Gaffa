How to Read It

Three clubs chase a centre-back and three a left-back, and you hold a spare left-back and no spare centre-back. Read across the LB row to see exactly who you’d sell to, and that Matchday Militia named Kerkez instead of asking for the position — which makes them the most serious of the three.

Read down Kingsmead’s column: a centre-back and a striker. They’re rebuilding a spine, not shopping.

RowsThe twelve tactical positions, in spine order.
ColumnsEvery club, yours ruled off in the middle.
LettersOn a named target you see the man’s initial; on a role, its level — S for starter, B for bench, P for prospect.
